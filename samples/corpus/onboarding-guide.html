New Starter Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Fernhollow Instruments. This guide walks you through your first weeks. Your manager and your onboarding buddy will help you with each step.

Before your first day

People Operations sends your contract and a welcome pack about two weeks before you start. Please return the signed contract, a copy of your identity document and your bank details through the HR portal at least 5 working days before your start date, so that you are paid on time.

Your first day

Arrive at the Eastbridge reception at 09:00 on your first day (Port Sallow starters arrive at 09:30). Reception gives you a temporary visitor badge; your photo ID badge is printed by Facilities on the same morning.

  • 09:00 Welcome and badge photo with Facilities
  • 10:00 Laptop collection and account setup with the IT Service Desk
  • 12:00 Lunch with your team (paid by the company)
  • 14:00 Meet your onboarding buddy
  • 15:30 First one-to-one with your manager

Your onboarding buddy

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from another team for their first 3 months. Your buddy is your first contact for practical questions, such as where things are and who to ask. Buddies meet you at least once a week during your first month.

Mandatory training

Complete these courses in the learning portal within 30 days of your start date:

  1. Security awareness (45 minutes)
  2. Data protection basics (30 minutes)
  3. Code of conduct and Speak Up (20 minutes)
  4. Fire safety and first aid orientation (25 minutes, in person, every second Tuesday)

Laboratory and manufacturing staff also complete the electrostatic discharge (ESD) handling course before working on the production floor.

Equipment

Office staff receive a laptop, a headset and a company phone. If you need a second monitor or an ergonomic keyboard, ask the IT Service Desk; requests are fulfilled within 10 working days.
