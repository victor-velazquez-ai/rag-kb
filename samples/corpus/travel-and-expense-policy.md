# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy reference FIN-07. Owner: Finance. Effective 1 March 2026. This policy covers business travel and the expenses employees of Fernhollow Instruments may claim back.

## Booking travel

All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the internal Travel Desk at least 14 days before departure, unless the trip is urgent and approved by a director. The Travel Desk can be reached at travel@fernhollow.example. Bookings made privately are reimbursed only up to the price the Travel Desk would have paid.

### Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. For a single flight of 6 hours or longer, premium economy is allowed. Business class is never reimbursed, even when you pay the difference with loyalty points. Choose the lowest fare that fits the meeting schedule and allow free changes only when the trip is likely to move.

### Trains and cars

Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys under 500 km. Standard class is the default; first class is allowed for journeys over 3 hours. Using your own car for business is reimbursed at EUR 0.30 per kilometre, and parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Rental cars must be compact or mid-size.

## Hotels

Hotel costs are capped per night, including breakfast and taxes, by city tier:

| City tier | Examples | Nightly cap |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Capital cities and major financial centres | EUR 210 |
| Tier 2 | Other cities with more than 500,000 people | EUR 150 |
| Tier 3 | All other locations | EUR 110 |

If the only available hotel near the meeting is above the cap, ask the Travel Desk for an exception before booking.

## Meals and per diem

Instead of meal receipts, travellers receive a daily allowance (per diem): EUR 55 per day for domestic travel and EUR 70 per day for international travel. On the day of departure and the day of return the per diem is 50 percent. Meals paid by a customer or included in a conference ticket are deducted from the per diem. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Expense claims

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not paid. Attach a receipt for every item over EUR 25. Claims are paid with the next monthly payroll once approved.

Approval limits:

- Up to EUR 2,000 per claim: your line manager approves.
- Above EUR 2,000 per claim: a director approves.
- Claims by directors: the Chief Financial Officer approves.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times a year may request a corporate card from Finance. The card has a monthly limit of EUR 5,000. Personal use of the corporate card is not allowed, and every transaction must be matched to a receipt in the finance portal within 30 days.

## Questions

Questions about this policy go to Finance at finance@fernhollow.example.
