# FH-200 Air Quality Monitor: Frequently Asked Questions

The FH-200 is Fernhollow's indoor and outdoor air quality monitor. It measures particulate matter (PM1, PM2.5 and PM10), carbon dioxide, temperature, relative humidity and total volatile organic compounds, and sends readings to the Fernhollow Cloud dashboard.

## Power and battery

### How long does the battery last?

On the standard sampling interval of one reading every 5 minutes, the internal battery lasts about 18 months. On the high-frequency interval of one reading per minute it lasts about 9 months. The dashboard warns you when the battery drops below 15 percent.

### Can the FH-200 run on mains power?

Yes. Use the USB-C power adapter (sold separately, SKU FH-PSU-15). On mains power the battery acts as a backup for up to 72 hours during a power cut.

## Measurements

### What is the measurement range for PM2.5?

The PM2.5 sensor measures from 0 to 1,000 micrograms per cubic metre, with an accuracy of plus or minus 5 micrograms or 10 percent, whichever is greater.

### How often does the monitor need calibration?

Calibrate the FH-200 every 12 months. The carbon dioxide sensor calibrates itself automatically if the monitor sees fresh outdoor air at least once a week. Calibration can be ordered as a service (SKU FH-CAL-01); the unit is returned within 10 business days.

## Installation

### Can I install it outdoors?

Yes. The FH-200 is rated IP65, which protects it against dust and water jets. It operates from minus 20 to plus 50 degrees Celsius. Mount it at least 1.5 metres above the ground and away from direct exhaust or air vents.

### How does it connect to the network?

The FH-200 connects over Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz only). Sites without Wi-Fi can use the LTE modem kit (SKU FH-LTE-02).

## Data and software

### How do I export my data?

In the Fernhollow Cloud dashboard, open the device, choose Export, and download readings as CSV or JSON. Data is kept for 24 months on the Standard plan and 60 months on the Pro plan.

### How do firmware updates work?

Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fernhollow Cloud. Monitors check for updates every night at 02:00 local time. You can delay an update by up to 14 days from the dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

### How do I reset the monitor?

To restart the monitor, press the button once. To reset it to factory settings, hold the button for 10 seconds until the light blinks orange three times. A factory reset removes the Wi-Fi settings but keeps the stored readings.

### What does a red light mean?

A steady red light means the monitor cannot reach Fernhollow Cloud. Check the Wi-Fi network first. A blinking red light means a sensor fault; contact support and quote the device serial number.

## Warranty

The FH-200 has a 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects. An extended warranty adds 2 more years (SKU FH-EXT-2Y). Damage from water entering through an open service cover is not covered.
