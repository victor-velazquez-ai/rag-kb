# Customer Return (RMA) Procedure

Procedure reference OPS-11. Owner: Customer Support. This procedure describes how Customer Support handles a customer who wants to return an FH-200 monitor or an accessory. RMA stands for return merchandise authorisation.

## Scope

The procedure covers returns of products bought directly from Fernhollow Instruments. Products bought from a reseller are returned through that reseller.

## Steps

1. The customer contacts Customer Support by email at support@fernhollow.example or through the support form in Fernhollow Cloud, and gives the device serial number and the reason for the return.
2. Within 2 business days, the support agent checks the serial number in the order system and confirms that the product is within its warranty or within the 30-day return window.
3. The support agent asks the customer to run the remote diagnostic from the dashboard. If the diagnostic finds a configuration problem, the agent helps fix it and closes the case without a return.
4. If a return is needed, the agent issues an RMA number in the format RMA-YYYY-NNNNN (for example RMA-2026-00417) and emails the customer a prepaid shipping label.
5. The customer ships the product in its original packaging, or in packaging that protects it equally well, within 14 days. RMA numbers expire after 14 days.
6. The warehouse inspects the returned unit within 3 business days of arrival and records the result against the RMA number.
7. For a defective unit under warranty, the warehouse ships a replacement within 10 business days of arrival. For a non-defective unit returned within the 30-day window, Finance refunds the purchase price minus a 15 percent restocking fee.
8. The support agent closes the case and sends the customer a short satisfaction survey.

## Exceptions

Units with physical damage caused by the customer are not covered by the warranty. The customer can choose a paid repair (quoted after inspection) or have the unit returned at their own cost. Returns of more than 20 units from one customer need approval from the Head of Customer Support before an RMA number is issued.

## Records

Every RMA case is logged in the support system, and inspection results are kept for 3 years.
