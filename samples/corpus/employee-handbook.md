# Employee Handbook

Fernhollow Instruments designs and builds air quality monitors for schools, offices and city councils. This handbook explains how we work together. It applies to every employee at the Eastbridge headquarters and the Port Sallow office. Policy owner: People Operations. Version 4.2, effective 1 January 2026.

## Working hours

Our standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday, and everyone should be available for meetings during core hours. Outside core hours you may start and finish when it suits you, as long as your team knows your pattern.

Overtime must be agreed in advance with your manager. Paid overtime applies only to roles in manufacturing and field service, at 1.5 times the hourly rate on weekdays and 2 times the hourly rate on Sundays and public holidays. Other roles take time off in lieu within three months.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus the public holidays listed in the office holiday calendar. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. After five full years of service, annual leave rises to 30 days.

Request leave in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day off. Managers respond within 3 working days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken by 31 March; any other unused days expire on 31 December.

### Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence. You may self-certify absences of up to 3 consecutive calendar days. From the fourth day you need a note from a doctor. Employees receive full pay for up to 20 days of sickness absence in any rolling 12-month period.

### Parental leave

The primary carer receives 20 weeks of parental leave at full pay, followed by up to 12 further weeks at half pay. The secondary carer receives 6 weeks at full pay, which may be taken at any time in the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Tell People Operations at least 8 weeks before you plan to start parental leave.

### Other leave

- Bereavement leave: up to 5 days at full pay for a close family member, 2 days for others.
- Volunteering leave: 2 paid days per year for registered charity work.
- Jury service: full pay for the duration of the service.
- Moving house: 1 paid day per year.

## Remote work

Most office roles may work remotely for up to 3 days per week under a signed Remote Work Agreement. Manufacturing, laboratory and field service roles are office or site based. Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year and needs written approval from People Operations at least 4 weeks in advance, because of tax and insurance rules.

The company provides a one-time home office allowance of EUR 300 for a chair, desk or monitor when your Remote Work Agreement is first approved.

## Probation and notice

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks on either side. After probation the notice period is 1 month for employees with less than 3 years of service and 3 months for employees with 3 or more years of service.

## Conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, to People Operations, or anonymously through the Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider and answered within 5 working days.

Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than EUR 50 must be declared in the gifts register within 7 days. Gifts worth more than EUR 150 must be declined or handed to People Operations.
