      How answers are produced

      1. Search. Your question is matched against every passage in the collection twice: by meaning (vector search on local embeddings) and by words (full-text search). The two ranked lists are fused, near-duplicates are dropped, and the best passages are kept (the Passages setting on the Ask tab).
      2. Compose. The kept passages are numbered and sent, with your question, to the provider you chose. It is told to answer only from those passages, in the language of your question, direct answer first, citing each sentence with a number like [2].
      3. Check. Citations are renumbered 1, 2, 3 in order of use and mapped to the sources list. When the passages do not answer the question, the provider replies with a fixed signal and you see a "Not covered" state instead of a guess, with the closest passages underneath so you can judge for yourself.

      How to read the scores

      The rank score orders the sources; the similarity and reranker scores say how relevant each one is.

      • Similarity (the number and the bar) is the cosine similarity between the meaning of your question and the meaning of the passage, from 0 to 1. It is on the same scale for every answer, so a full bar is a close match in any answer. What counts as high depends on the embedding model: compare sources with each other, and learn what a good match looks like on your own documents. A passage found only by the word search has no similarity score.
      • Reranker appears when the local reranker is on (RAGKB_RERANKER=local). A second model reads the question and the passage together; higher means more relevant.
      • Rank comes from rank fusion: a passage that ranks high in both the meaning search and the word search comes first. Typical values are small (around 0.01 to 0.04). It is a ranking signal, not a probability and not a measure of truth.
      • Low similarity across all sources usually means the collection does not cover the question well. Open the passage to check what was actually said.

      Documents and uploads

      • The count next to a collection, and in the header, is the number of searchable (ready) documents. Documents still processing, failed or cancelled are listed after it as a note.
      • Each upload shows its stage (reading, splitting, embedding, writing), how many passages are done and about how long is left. Cancel stops a queued or running job; the earlier version of a document, if any, stays searchable.
      • A failed document offers Retry (index it again from the kept original) and Dismiss (remove it).
      • Add folder uploads every supported file in a folder, and a .zip is unpacked into one document per file. Each keeps its path, so two files with the same name in different folders stay two documents.

      Keyboard and links

      • / jumps to the question box. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line.
      • On the tab bar, Left and Right move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends.
      • The drop zone opens the file picker with Enter or Space.
      • Delete and Dismiss buttons need a second click within 4 seconds.
      • The browser's Back and Forward buttons move between tabs and collections. The address of a tab, or of an answer, opens the same view again after a reload or from a bookmark.

      Privacy

      Documents, embeddings and the index stay in the data folder on this machine. Only the passages chosen for an answer leave it, inside the prompt sent to the provider you picked, which is the same as pasting them into that assistant yourself. The audit log keeps who answered, how long it took and the outcome, never the passages or the answer text.
