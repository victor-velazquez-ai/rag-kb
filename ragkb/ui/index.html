      Settings and health

      Settings come from RAGKB_* environment variables or a .env file next to where you start the server. Change them there and restart.

      Connect your assistant

      Your own assistant can use this knowledge base as a tool through MCP. It searches and reads the brief here, then writes the answer on your own subscription.

      How answers are produced

      1. Search. Your question is matched against every passage in the collection twice: by meaning (vector search on local embeddings) and by words (full-text search). The two ranked lists are fused, near-duplicates are dropped, and the best passages are kept (the Passages setting on the Ask tab).
      2. Compose. The kept passages are numbered and sent, with your question, to the provider you chose. It is told to answer only from those passages, in the language of your question, direct answer first, citing each sentence with a number like [2].
      3. Check. Citations are renumbered 1, 2, 3 in order of use and mapped to the sources list. When the passages do not answer the question, the provider replies with a fixed signal and you see a "Not covered" state instead of a guess.

      Providers

      • Claude Code runs on your Claude Pro or Max plan. Sign in once with claude and then /login.
      • Codex runs on your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan. Sign in once with codex login.
      • Extractive mode uses no AI: it lists the best sentences from the top passages with their citations. It always works, also with no subscription.

      Auto picks Claude Code when it is signed in, then Codex, then extractive mode.

      How to read the scores

      • Each source shows a score and a bar. The score comes from rank fusion: a passage that ranks high in both the meaning search and the word search scores highest. It is a ranking signal, not a probability and not a measure of truth.
      • The bar is relative to the strongest source in the same answer, so a full bar means "the best match here", not "a perfect match".
      • Low scores across the board usually mean the collection does not cover the question well. Open the passage to check what was actually said.

      Keyboard

      • / jumps to the question box. Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a line.
      • On the tab bar, Left and Right move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends.
      • The drop zone opens the file picker with Enter or Space.
      • Delete buttons need a second click within 4 seconds.

      Privacy

      Documents, embeddings and the index stay in the data folder on this machine. Only the passages chosen for an answer leave it, inside the prompt sent to the provider you picked, which is the same as pasting them into that assistant yourself. The audit log keeps who answered, how long it took and the outcome, never the passages or the answer text.
